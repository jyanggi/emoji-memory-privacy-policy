# Privacy Policy

**Wumble Scramble** ("we", "our", or "us") operates the **Wumble Scramble** mobile application (the "Service").

This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service and the choices you have associated with that data.

**Information Collection and Use**

We do not collect, use, or disclose any personal data from users of our Service. Wumble Scramble is a word game where you unscramble falling words and learn what they mean. It does not require an account and does not connect to the internet: all the words and their meanings are built into the app.

The game stores a small amount of information **only on your device**, so it can remember where you are:

- the player names you type in and the avatar picked for each player,
- an optional 4-digit PIN for a player, if you choose to set one,
- each player's game progress: scores, stars, unlocked levels and badges,
- each player's Word Book: the words they have solved or missed, and any words they starred,
- whether sound effects and music are switched on or off.

This information never leaves your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting a player in the game removes that player's information, including their Word Book, and uninstalling the app removes all of it.

**Log Data**

We do not collect any log data or usage data from our users.

**Cookies**

Our Service does not use cookies or any other tracking technologies.

**Advertising and Analytics**

Our Service does not show advertisements and does not use analytics or crash-reporting services.

**Service Providers**

We do not employ third-party companies or individuals to facilitate our Service, provide the Service on our behalf, perform Service-related services, or assist us in analyzing how our Service is used.

**Device Permissions**

Wumble Scramble does not ask for access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts or files. It only plays sounds, music and short vibrations, which can be turned off in the game.

**Children's Privacy**

Our Service is suitable for children of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children. Wumble Scramble only stores player names, game progress, Word Books and settings locally on the device, ensuring a safe and private experience for all users, including children. A parent can add a PIN so that only the right child can open or change their player.

**Changes to This Privacy Policy**

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes. Changes to this Privacy Policy are effective when they are posted on this page.
